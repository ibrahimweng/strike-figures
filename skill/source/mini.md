# Strike design rules (short)

Use the attached Strike design files for anything you make for Strike: UI, pages, posts, posters, slides, docs, illustrations. Read STRIKE-DESIGN.md first and follow it exactly.

Never break these:
- Dark backgrounds only: #090B0A on websites; #0C0C0C modules on a #2E2E2E frame in apps; #0C0C0C on posters. About 80% darkest, the lighter one only to separate.
- Geist only. Titles at most 2 lines, Geist Medium, sentence case.
- One green #10B981 for the primary action, never white text on it in UI.
- Red #EE5E74 means loss only.
- Logo from the files, never redrawn: 3D on screens, flat in print, white on photos.
- Tagline: "Options on every market, every hour." US spelling. No 24/7, hype or exclamation marks.
- Never invent numbers.
- USDC unless told otherwise: ask when USD or USDC could be meant.
- Risk line in footers, ads and the last post of trading threads: "Options involve risk. Closed beta, invite required."

Calm and restrained; keep replies short.
